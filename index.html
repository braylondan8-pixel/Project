Frequently Asked Questions

What is AI Foundry? +

AI Foundry is a community and platform that empowers AI innovators, entrepreneurs, and experts to collaborate, learn, and grow. We provide exclusive resources, mentorship programs, networking opportunities, and funding support to help AI startups succeed.

Who can join the community? +

Anyone passionate about AI, startups, product development, or entrepreneurship can join. We welcome founders, builders, researchers, and investors.

Do you offer mentorship and support? +

Yes. Members gain access to mentorship, guidance, learning resources, and strategic connections designed to help them build and scale AI ventures.

How do I get started? +

Simply sign up on our platform, join the community, and explore the resources, events, and mentorship opportunities available to members.
